# Water Quality Research

## 📄 Research Paper

[Water Quality HR-DNN Research Paper](Water_Quality_HR_DNN_Research_Paper.pdf)

## 📝 Abstract

This research presents a machine learning and deep learning-based approach for water quality assessment and prediction using relevant water quality parameters.

## 🎯 Research Objective

To develop an efficient data-driven model for analyzing water quality and improving prediction accuracy.

## 🔬 Methodology

The study involves data preprocessing, feature analysis, model development, training, evaluation, and performance comparison using statistical and machine learning techniques.

## 📊 Key Results

The developed models demonstrated strong predictive performance and showed the potential of deep learning for reliable water quality assessment.
